# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want Ivorie to be a harassment-free space for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good behavior:
- Be kind and respectful.
- Accept feedback with grace and give it with care.
- Focus on what is best for the project and community.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.

Not acceptable:
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Sharing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that break this code. They may ban a person for behavior they judge harmful.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report abuse to the maintainers at marvelousdev99@gmail.com. All reports are reviewed, kept confidential, and answered. Maintainers must respect the privacy of the reporter.

## Enforcement steps

1. **Correction:** private note and a request for a public apology if fitting.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences if the behavior continues.
3. **Temporary ban:** no interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban:** for a pattern of violations or serious harm.
